# Interview guide for interviews 2 and 3

Goal: hear from the two people Interview 1 says are affected, the **employee who submits late** and the **manager** whose team's charges get auto-approved. About 10 minutes each. Write the answers in `interview-2.md` and `interview-3.md` in your own words. Use a title or initials, never a name.

Ask about something that really happened ("last time"), not opinions about the future. Write down numbers when they give them.

## Interview 2: an employee who has submitted an expense report late

1. Tell me about the last time you turned in an expense report late. What happened?
2. When the system's reminder email came, where were you and what did you do with it?
3. Where do you actually see messages you act on: Teams, text, or email?
4. What's the most annoying part of doing an expense report? Finding receipts, the website, the time it takes, something else?
5. Have you ever just not submitted something small? Roughly how much money was it?
6. What would have gotten you to submit on time that last time?
7. *(Tests the riskiest assumption.)* If I had sent you a Teams message or text listing your open charges and how many days you had left, would you have done it sooner? Why or why not?

## Interview 3: a manager whose team submits expense reports

1. When one of your people is late on an expense report, how do you find out today?
2. Tell me about the last time it happened. What did you do?
3. Do you know when charges get auto-approved without a receipt? What does that cost the company or your budget?
4. Would you want a heads-up before your team's charges get auto-approved? How, and how many days before?
5. What would you *not* want to see? For example, details of other teams, or messages every day.
6. Who else cares about this: your director, finance, audit?
