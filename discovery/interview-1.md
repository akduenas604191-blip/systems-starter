# Interview 1: Expense Compliance Specialist (Finance / Accounts Payable)

Names removed. Notes as written by the interviewer, transcribed from `BMIS_301_Interviews.docx`.

**Interviewee:** Expense Compliance Specialist  
**Department:** Finance / Accounts Payable  
**Interview Objective:** Identify challenges associated with expense report submissions, deadline management, and compliance.

**Interviewer: Tell me about a time you dealt with missed deadlines.**

Interviewee: Employees would frequently miss deadlines when submitting their expense reports. This would eventually trigger the system's automatic approval process, completely bypassing the compliance measures put in place. As a result, expenses could be approved without proper documentation, receipts, or review to ensure they were in accordance with company policy.

**Interviewer: What was the hardest part?**

Interviewee: The hardest part was finding an easier and more effective way to encourage employees to submit their expense reports on time. It was difficult to balance holding employees accountable while making the submission process convenient for them and minimizing the additional workload for our department.

**Interviewer: How often does it happen?**

Interviewee: It happens quite often, particularly toward the end of each month. There are consistently employees who miss their deadlines, making it a recurring issue rather than an isolated incident.

**Interviewer: What have you tried that does not work?**

Interviewee: We have tried reaching out through email, both through automated system reminders and individual follow-up emails. However, email has not been particularly effective in encouraging timely expense report submissions. Employees often overlook the reminders or become distracted by their other daily responsibilities.

**Interviewer: How much time does it cost you?**

Interviewee: It costs me a significant amount of time each month. Once the system flags an overdue expense report and sends an automated reminder, I have to monitor whether the employee takes action. If they do not, I have to reach out to them individually and sometimes follow up multiple times. This takes time away from my other responsibilities and creates unnecessary repetitive work.

**Interviewer: Who else is involved and affected?**

Interviewee: The employees, finance department, and department managers are all affected. Employees who submit reimbursement reports risk not receiving money they have personally spent on business-related expenses. The finance department is affected because missed deadlines increase administrative work and create compliance risks. Managers are also affected because they may need to intervene when their employees repeatedly fail to submit reports.

**Interviewer: If you could wave a wand, what would be different?**

Interviewee: Ideally, I would eliminate the need for additional manual reminders and individual follow-ups. I would want a system that effectively notifies employees of upcoming deadlines, automatically escalates overdue reports when necessary, and makes it easier for employees to complete their submissions. This would allow us to maintain our compliance standards, reduce administrative work, and ensure employees receive their reimbursements in a timely manner.
