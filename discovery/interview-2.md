# Interview 2: Donor Relations Representative (an employee who submits expense reports)

**Interviewee:** Donor relations representative  
**Department:** Donor relations  
**Interview Objective:** Understand the challenges employees experience when submitting expense reports and meeting deadlines.

**Interviewer: Tell me about a time you dealt with missed deadlines.**

Interviewee: There have been several occasions when I missed the deadline for submitting my expense reports. Between traveling for work, attending meetings, and managing my daily responsibilities, I would often forget to submit my expenses. There have also been instances where I missed out on reimbursements for expenses I paid for personally.

**Interviewer: What was the hardest part?**

Interviewee: The hardest part was keeping track of receipts and remembering when my expense reports were due. By the time I had an opportunity to complete my reports, I would sometimes have difficulty locating receipts or remembering the business purpose of certain transactions.

**Interviewer: How often does it happen?**

Interviewee: It happens fairly often, especially during months when I am traveling frequently or have a heavier workload.

**Interviewer: What have you tried that does not work?**

Interviewee: I have tried relying on email reminders and setting personal calendar reminders. However, the emails often get buried, and I sometimes dismiss calendar notifications because I am working on something else at the time.

**Interviewer: How much time does it cost you?**

Interviewee: It takes a considerable amount of time, especially when I allow multiple expenses to accumulate. I have to go back through my transactions, locate receipts, and remember the details of each purchase. It becomes more time-consuming the longer I put it off.

**Interviewer: Who else is involved and affected?**

Interviewee: The finance department is affected because they have to spend additional time reminding me to submit my reports. My manager is also affected because they are responsible for reviewing and approving my expenses. Personally, I am affected when my reimbursements are delayed.

**Interviewer: If you could wave a wand, what would be different?**

Interviewee: I would make it easier to submit expenses immediately after making a purchase. Having the ability to upload receipts from my phone, receive more noticeable reminders, and easily see upcoming deadlines would help me stay on top of my expense reports without needing someone from finance to reach out personally.
