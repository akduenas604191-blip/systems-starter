# Interview 3: Department Manager (Operations)

Names removed. Notes as written by the interviewer, transcribed from `BMIS_301_Interviews.docx`.

**Interviewee:** Department Manager  
**Department:** Operations  
**Interview Objective:** Understand the challenges managers experience when overseeing employee expense report compliance and approvals.

**Interviewer: Tell me about a time you dealt with missed deadlines.**

Interviewee: There have been multiple instances where employees within my department failed to submit their expense reports on time. I would typically become aware of the issue after receiving an email from the finance department requesting that I follow up with the employees. In some situations, expenses would be automatically approved before I had an opportunity to properly review them.

**Interviewer: What was the hardest part?**

Interviewee: The hardest part was not having enough visibility into which employees had outstanding expense reports. I was dependent on the finance department notifying me of the issue rather than being able to identify and address it before the deadline passed.

**Interviewer: How often does it happen?**

Interviewee: It happens regularly, particularly around the end of the month. While it is not always the same employees, there are consistently outstanding reports that require additional attention.

**Interviewer: What have you tried that does not work?**

Interviewee: I have tried sending department-wide email reminders, bringing up expense report deadlines during team meetings, and reaching out to employees individually. Although these methods occasionally work, they have not provided a consistent long-term solution.

**Interviewer: How much time does it cost you?**

Interviewee: It takes time away from my primary responsibilities as a manager. I have to review communications from finance, identify the employees with outstanding reports, contact them individually, and follow up to ensure the reports have been submitted. Much of this work feels repetitive and could potentially be automated.

**Interviewer: Who else is involved and affected?**

Interviewee: The finance department, employees, department managers, and company leadership are all affected. Finance has to dedicate additional resources to enforcing compliance, employees risk missing reimbursements, and managers have less oversight of departmental spending. The company is also exposed to potential compliance and financial risks when expenses are automatically approved without proper review.

**Interviewer: If you could wave a wand, what would be different?**

Interviewee: I would want a centralized system that allows me to see which employees have outstanding expense reports and when their deadlines are approaching. I would also want the system to automatically remind employees and notify me before expenses reach automatic approval. This would allow me to intervene when necessary without constantly monitoring or reminding employees myself.
